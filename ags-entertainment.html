Our story

Started by three brothers with one film, AGS Entertainment has grown into one of Chennai's most consistent producers of commercial Tamil cinema — still family-run, still picking scripts over spectacle.

2006

The first film

Founded in Chennai by brothers Kalpathi S. Aghoram, Kalpathi S. Ganesh and Kalpathi S. Suresh. Debut production Thiruttu Payale opens to a strong commercial response.

2010–14

A house style

A run of script-driven hits — Avan Ivan, Madrasapattinam, Maattrraan — builds a reputation for backing directors with a clear voice.

2015–17

Into exhibition

AGS Cinemas opens, bringing four screens to Chennai, alongside continued production — Thani Oruvan, Anegan, Kavan.

2019

Bigil

A collaboration with director Atlee becomes one of Tamil cinema's biggest theatrical events of the year.

Today

28 films and counting

From Dragon to Blast, still run day-to-day by the Kalpathi family, with a slate of new productions in the works.

A Chennai-based film production, distribution and exhibition house, producing Tamil cinema since 2006.
